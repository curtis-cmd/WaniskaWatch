# Conservative public-record eligibility

Public records are verified against cited government sources as of their displayed
dates, not guaranteed real-time or individually confirmed against every registry entry.

- A past listed expiry/due date is withheld even if a generic current status remains.
  This is an unresolved publication conflict, not a finding that the title is invalid.
- Records need a current source status or a valid unexpired date. Operating mines
  also require a positive operating status. Missing evidence is not verified zero activity.
- Numeric holder values are source identifiers, not resolved names. Keep identifiers
  in a separate field and flag name resolution without inventing a company or person.
- Refresh daily. Mining verification expires after 48 hours; a boundary outage does
  not extend that window. The production asset guard blocks stale/unavailable data.
- Only audited datasets may contribute to province counters, national summaries or
  detailed tiles. Rebuild all derived layers as one tested release.
- Retain raw evidence and correction outcomes privately. A withheld record can return
  after a passing source refresh resolves its status/date conflict. Do not invent a
  renewal, overwrite source dates or describe excluded holders as wrongdoers.

This policy is not legal clearance. Source-specific licence terms, privacy questions
and notices remain separate review requirements; technical eligibility does not
grant republication rights or guarantee accuracy.
